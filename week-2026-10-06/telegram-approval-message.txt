LinkedIn caption:
When a service failure happens, customers do not only judge the outcome.

They judge the fairness of the recovery.

Did someone take ownership?
Was the explanation clear?
Was the correction proportionate?
Was the customer treated with respect while the issue was handled?

That is why service recovery is not a soft skill. It is governance.

The research on perceived justice in service recovery is useful because it separates the recovery into three practical questions:

Outcome: what did we do to fix it?
Process: how easy was it for the customer to get help?
Interaction: how did we make the customer feel while fixing it?

In mystery shopping, my team looks for all three.

A refund with a cold tone can still damage confidence.
A warm apology with no correction can still feel empty.
A good process with no owner can still feel exhausting.

The point is not to recover every mistake with generosity. The point is to recover with discipline.

Because after a failure, the customer is not asking whether the brand is perfect.

They are asking whether the brand is fair.

2. Thursday post - Data Drop: Closed Is Not Recovered
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/captainredbaron/social_media/main/week-2026-10-06/thursday-data-drop-recovery-justice.jpg

Caption:
Data Drop: complaint closure is not the same as customer recovery.

Many operations track whether the case was closed.

That is useful, but incomplete.

A closed case can still leave the customer feeling that the process was slow, the explanation was thin, or the ownership was unclear.

That is why I prefer recovery measurement to include three separate checks:

1. Outcome - was the correction appropriate?
2. Process - was it easy to reach a resolution?
3. Interaction - was the customer treated with respect?

When those are combined into one generic "resolved" status, leadership loses the diagnostic value.

The danger is obvious: the dashboard shows closure, while the customer remembers effort.

Mystery shopping helps separate these signals. It shows whether recovery is governed as a behavior, not just logged as a ticket.

Closed is an internal status.

Recovered is the customer's judgement.
