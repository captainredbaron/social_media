## Tuesday Carousel (The Halo Effect)
**Caption:**
Does a great greeting excuse a slow kitchen?

Psychologist Edward Thorndike called it The Halo Effect (1920) — the cognitive bias where one positive trait overshadows everything else.

In Customer Experience, we see it in the data every week:
When the first interaction is brilliant, guests become forgiving.
When the first interaction is cold, they become critics — noticing every speck of dust and counting every second of delay.

You can't fix every operational flaw overnight. But you CAN anchor the first impression.
What is your brand's halo?
